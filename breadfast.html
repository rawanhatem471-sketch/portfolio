Breadfast — Spec Ad

Campaign Objective

To increase sales by showing that Breadfast is not only for breakfast, but a fast and convenient option for different moments throughout the day.

Insight

Many people associate Breadfast mainly with breakfast, while in reality, it offers much more than just breakfast. At the same time, we all procrastinate and make excuses to put things off. Sometimes, one small excuse is enough to delay everything—like saying you cannot study because you have not had your coffee yet.

Big Idea

No More Excuses.

Concept

A sarcastic and fast-paced comedy ad about a high school student who keeps making excuses to avoid studying for her upcoming exams.
